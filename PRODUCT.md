# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal IWS Finserv staff and analysts. These are professionals who use this portal as a daily work tool to view and manage portfolio data, review client information, and monitor system status. They work at desks, often across multiple screens, and expect the interface to get out of the way so they can focus on the data.

## Product Purpose

A Management Information System (MIS) portal for IWS Finserv. Currently covers authentication and user session management, with a portfolio dashboard in progress. The portal is the internal operational surface through which analysts access client and portfolio data. Success means: fast access to accurate information, no friction in daily workflows, and an interface trustworthy enough that no one questions whether the data is right.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, composed, precise. The portal commands trust through clarity, not decoration. It feels like a tool built by people who understand finance, not just design. Approachable in the sense that daily use is never exhausting, but never casual. Think Linear meets institutional finance.

## Anti-references

- Navy + gold fintech cliché: the standard "professional finance" palette every competitor defaults to
- Consumer banking UI: rounded, bubbly, gradient-heavy — feels like a retail mobile app
- Legacy enterprise (SharePoint era): accordion-heavy, gray, cluttered, no visual hierarchy
- Crypto / DeFi aesthetic: dark with neon glow effects, speculative and aggressive in tone

## Design Principles

1. **Information first.** Every layout decision serves data legibility. Decoration earns its place or gets cut.
2. **Calm authority.** Serious without being cold. Precise without being sterile. The interface implies competence.
3. **Considered restraint.** One accent color. Consistent spacing. Nothing that draws attention to itself.
4. **Consistent legibility.** Analysts read this portal for hours. Typography and contrast must hold up at sustained use, not just on first glance.
5. **Zero ambiguity.** Labels are exact. States are unambiguous. Error messages say what happened and what to do.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Keyboard navigation throughout. Minimum 4.5:1 contrast for text. Reduced motion respect via `prefers-reduced-motion`. No color-only state signaling.
