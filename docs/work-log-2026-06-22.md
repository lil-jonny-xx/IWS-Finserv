# Worklog — 2026-06-22

## Realised Gains — periods, foreign FX, and PMS

- **Period & switch toggles:** the Realised Gains page can toggle FY-to-date vs since-inception, and include vs exclude `SWITCH_IN`/`SWITCH_OUT`. `_fetch_realised_gains` gains `since_inception`/`include_switches` kwargs (defaults preserve prior behaviour and the XLSX report); the endpoint forwards `?period=` and `?switches=`.
- **Foreign-equity realisations at trade-date FX:** new `_fx_rate_on()` date-aware currency→INR lookup (nearest rate on/before) + a foreign-equity branch in `_fetch_realised_gains` reading `equity_trade_ledger`, converting each leg's native cash flow to INR at its own trade-date rate so realised P&L captures both price and FX movement. New `fx_backfill_worker.py` backfills historical rates from frankfurter.app.
- **PMS realised P&L:** PMS holdings are snapshot-only, so realisations come from the broker's realised capital-gains statement. New `pms_realised` table + `import_pms_realised.py` fuzzy CSV/XLSX importer, wired into `_fetch_realised_gains` as `group='PMS'` and honouring the period toggle.

- PMS total invested = equity cost basis + cash parked in the account (cash is uninvested principal).
